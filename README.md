## Business Problem Statement

Retail email campaigns are often sent to broad customer segments, resulting in marketing 
spend on customers who would have made a purchase even without receiving the email. In some 
cases, contacting the wrong customers can actively reduce purchase likelihood, making precise 
targeting not just a matter of efficiency but of avoiding negative campaign effects. This 
reduces campaign efficiency and return on investment. The business challenge is to identify 
persuadable customers—those whose purchasing decision is directly influenced by the email—so 
that marketing efforts are targeted only where they generate incremental sales: purchases that 
occur specifically because the email was sent and would not have occurred otherwise. This 
improves campaign effectiveness while reducing unnecessary costs.
